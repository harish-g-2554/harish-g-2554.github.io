Research Paper 📈 Financial Forecasting

Financial Market Forecasting: An Evaluation of Hybrid Models on the BSE Healthcare Index

Harish G - MSc Data Science Research
CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru, Karnataka
Dataset: April 2017 - March 2025 | BSE Healthcare Index

Abstract

Forecasting stock market movements is difficult because financial time series are dynamic, nonlinear, and volatile. Recent progress in deep learning and the increasing availability of high-speed computers have increased the opportunities to effectively and accurately depict these complexities. Ensemble and hybrid modeling is one modern design that can handle massive amounts of historical data, reveal hidden temporal connections, and adjust to various data patterns. This research uses BSE healthcare data from April 2017 to March 2025. The series is first divided into trend, seasonal, and residual components to understand its structural behavior better.

Using this framework, we develop and assess five hybrid models: ETS + XGBoost, SARIMA + XGBoost, STL + XGBoost, Polynomial and Fourier + XGBoost, and Holt's Linear Trend + XGBoost for predicting the index's closing values. This study demonstrates hybrid models' capacity to identify intricate temporal patterns and offers data on how well they forecast financial markets.

Key Methodologies & Findings

  • Time series decomposition: Trend, Seasonal, and Residual component analysis
  • Developed and compared 5 hybrid models combining statistical and ML approaches
  • XGBoost integration for capturing complex non-linear patterns in financial data
  • Comprehensive evaluation of forecasting accuracy on BSE Healthcare sector
  • Demonstrated superiority of hybrid models over traditional statistical methods
